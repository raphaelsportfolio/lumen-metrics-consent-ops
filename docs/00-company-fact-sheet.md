## Why this scenario fits the project
- The product has clear, distinct marketing communication types (product
  updates, promotions, sales outreach), which is what makes granular,
  category-level consent worth demonstrating instead of a single flag.
- The company has real, current EU exposure in the scenario, which justifies
  treating GDPR as the operative baseline rather than an afterthought.
- Small team, no existing MOps discipline — a realistic reason a junior MOps
  hire would be asked to build exactly this system.
