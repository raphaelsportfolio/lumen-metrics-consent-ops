Cookie Policy

This page explains the categories of cookies this demo site uses, and how you can control them.

How we categorize cookies

Necessary cookies are required for the site to function and are never subject to a consent choice. Analytics cookies help us understand how visitors use the site, and only load after you actively grant consent through our cookie banner.
